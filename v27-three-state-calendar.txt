Version 2.7 - Three-State Calendar System
=========================================
Time: Monday Feb 24, 2026 at 8:25 PM PST

THREE-STATE CALENDAR COLORS:

1. PURPLE (rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3))
   - Days you're NOT supposed to do the habit
   - Example: Weekends for "Weekdays Only" habits
   - Still clickable to mark as bonus completion

2. RED (#ef4444)
   - Days you SHOULD do it but missed
   - Only appears on scheduled days

3. GREEN (#10b981)
   - Days you completed the habit
   - Works for both scheduled and bonus days

CLICKING LOGIC:

- Purple → Green: Did it on an off day (bonus!)
- Red → Green: Completed a missed day
- Green → Red: Mark incomplete (only on scheduled days)
- Green → Purple: Remove bonus completion (on unscheduled days)

SMART BEHAVIOR:
- Respects your habit frequency settings
- Weekdays Only: Mon-Fri are normal, Sat-Sun are purple
- Weekends Only: Sat-Sun are normal, Mon-Fri are purple
- Custom Days: Only selected days are normal, others are purple

VISUAL IMPROVEMENTS:
- Muted professional green instead of neon
- Better contrast and readability
- Clear visual distinction between states
