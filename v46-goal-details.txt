Version 4.6 - Goal Details Editor
=================================
Time: Tuesday Feb 25, 2026 at 9:35 AM PST

NEW FEATURE: Comprehensive Goal Editor

WHAT'S NEW:
1. Click any goal period (Yearly, Quarterly, Monthly, Weekly) to open the detailed editor
2. Each period can now have:
   - Notes: Add context, thoughts, or strategy
   - Links: Add multiple related URLs with names
   - Lead Indicators: What you'll DO (actions)
   - Lag Indicators: What you'll MEASURE (results)

HOW TO USE:
- Click on any goal period box (yearly/quarterly/monthly/weekly)
- Fill in the details in the modal that opens
- Add as many links as needed with the "+ Add Link" button
- Save to store all information

VISUAL INDICATORS:
- When a period has notes, links, or indicators, you'll see:
  - 📝 icon in the top-right corner of yearly goals
  - "📝 Has details" text below quarterly/monthly/weekly goals

EXAMPLE:
- Yearly Goal: "40% revenue growth"
  - Notes: "Focus on digital products and Alberta expansion"
  - Links: Analytics dashboard, Revenue tracker
  - Lead Indicators: "Launch 2 products, 50 cold calls/week"
  - Lag Indicators: "Monthly revenue, Customer acquisition cost"

All data is saved in localStorage and persists between sessions!
